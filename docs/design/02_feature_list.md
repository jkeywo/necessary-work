# The Necessary Work — Prototype Feature List

## Core objective

Reduce global gross greenhouse-gas emissions to zero while keeping ongoing Finance and Mandate deltas non-negative. The 1990 prototype cannot be lost.

## World structure

### Continents

- Europe
- North America
- Majority World — temporary prototype label requiring review before public release

Differences are authored starting systems and bottlenecks, not innate regional traits or development rankings.

### Sectors per continent

- Power
- Transport and Buildings
- Industry and Land

The prototype tracks nine sector emission rates. Baselines remain fixed unless changed by projects.

## Resources

### Finance

- One global stock, no maximum.
- Accumulates over time.
- Each continent's contribution is visible.
- Projects may add income, impose maintenance, reduce future costs, or improve spending efficiency.
- Modest positive baseline income guarantees recoverability.

### Mandate

- One global stock with current value, maximum, and income delta.
- Spent upfront to begin contentious or administratively demanding projects.
- Each continent contributes visibly to baseline income.
- Some projects increase income; many Mandate-building projects increase the maximum.
- Represents CFA treaty coordination authority, not local public consent.
- Positive baseline income guarantees recoverability.

## Capacity icons

Tracked separately for each continent:

- Knowledge
- Infrastructure
- Workforce
- Institutions

Icons are installed capacities, not spendable resources. Projects may explicitly use them for prerequisites, scaling, breakpoints, unlocks, duration, costs, effect upgrades, or spillovers. There is no generic capacity score and no automatic global pooling.

## Projects

Target: approximately 12 projects, weighted toward power.

Every project is data-defined through a controlled effect vocabulary.

Prototype activation profiles:

- Completion
- Linear rollout

Milestone activation is reserved for the vertical slice.

Repeat models:

- Unique
- Repeatable
- Tiered
- Capped rollout

Controlled cost curves:

- linear;
- exponential;
- economies of scale followed by depletion.

Each project specifies lead continent, Finance and Mandate costs, duration, slot requirement, prerequisites, icon outputs, scaling, breakpoints, scope/spillovers, sector effects, recurring income/upkeep, repeat model, activation profile, and context/source metadata.

### Construction cancellation

Cancelling an incomplete project destroys progress and partial rollout effects, gives no refund, and frees the slot.

### Decommissioning

Completed projects may be decommissioned immediately. Benefits, icons, spillovers, and upkeep disappear; no refund is given. Rebuild rules must prevent profitable toggling.

## Programme capacity

- Programme slots are global.
- Start with one.
- The CFA HQ is administratively located in Europe and gives no efficiency bonus.
- A second slot unlocks early when a continent reaches a visible Institutions milestone.
- A third unlocks around the midpoint through a later continental Institutions milestone.
- No dedicated slot-tax projects.

## Planning queue

- Strict FIFO.
- Add, remove, and reorder at any time.
- Resources are not reserved.
- The head begins when prerequisites, resources, and a slot are available.
- If blocked, the queue stalls and explains why.
- No automatic optimisation or production automation.

## Time and controls

- Real-time simulation at one authored speed.
- No player-facing fast-forward.
- Pause available.
- Full planning and inspection while paused.
- Project and research panels do not pause automatically.
- Debug builds include accelerated time controls.
- Headless tests can advance without wall-clock waiting.

## Opportunities

- Low-value beneficial events such as grants or conferences.
- No trade-off or minigame.
- Do not consume slots.
- Ignoring them is not punitive.
- Broad seeded timing windows.
- Timing and selection use separate deterministic random streams.
- Balance tests can disable them or use a fixed seed.
- Notifications persist.

## Project comparison

Selecting a project previews:

- immediate continental and global emissions changes;
- Finance and Mandate delta changes;
- icon totals after completion;
- breakpoints reached;
- newly unlocked projects;
- estimated time until affordable;
- active scaling calculation.

The preview separates guaranteed direct effects from conditional consequences. There is no combined efficiency score or automatic recommendation.

## Transparency

### Normal view

Headline effect, major icon contributions, important breakpoint, upkeep, and spillovers.

### Expanded explanation

Base value, all modifiers, stacking order, final result, evidence note, and game-abstraction note.

### Debug view

Complete state, full effect trace, event history, RNG streams, and command history.

All layers derive from the same calculation data.

## Interface

### Main dashboard

Global emissions and delta; Finance and Mandate with deltas; three continental summaries; active programmes; planning queue; available projects.

### Continental summary

Total emissions and delta; three sector values; four icon totals; Finance contribution; Mandate contribution; active programme indicator.

### Context side panel

Sector detail; icon breakdown; scaling and next breakpoint; real-world context; limits and trade-offs; abstraction; sources.

Targets native desktop and narrower WASM layouts.

## Completion and records

Victory triggers when gross global emissions are zero and Finance and Mandate deltas are non-negative.

Ranked time is active unpaused simulation time.

End screen shows completion time, provisional benchmark bracket, personal best, final deltas, replay validity, and a concise causal recap.

The score is time only.

## Explicitly excluded

Protection; Legitimacy; local consent; climate crises; adaptation; carbon removal; sector baseline growth; historical calendar; policy sliders; complex scripting; automation; degradation and random project failure; offline progress; online leaderboards; accounts; polished regional art; narrative chains; full educational assessment.
